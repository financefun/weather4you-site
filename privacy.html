Weather4You Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

The short version. Weather4You has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The app sends your coordinates to weather services to fetch forecasts and keeps your settings on your device. We never receive your location, your saved cities or anything that identifies you.

Who we are

Weather4You is an independent weather app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This policy covers the app, its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and its Apple Watch app and complications.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers that receive data from the app, and the app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software from us or anyone else. The only data about you that exists because of Weather4You lives on your devices, under your control.

Your location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show the weather where you are. Location is used only while the app or a widget is updating; the app never asks for background location. Your coordinates are sent to the weather services below to request a forecast, and the app may turn them into a place name on your device using Apple's geocoding so it can label the forecast. Your location is not stored by us and not shared with anyone else. The app keeps your most recent coordinates on your device so that widgets and the periodic background refresh can update without asking for a new fix. You can turn location off at any time in Settings, and the app keeps working with the cities you save.

Weather data providers

To show a forecast, the app sends the coordinates of your location or your saved city to these services. Each receives only what it needs to answer the request.

For the free app, every forecast, current reading and alert comes from the National Weather Service, which covers the United States. Apple Weather is used only with a Weather4You Pro subscription: for the Next Hour Rain feature and the rain chances it adds, and for cities outside the United States, which the National Weather Service does not cover.

  • National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), a United States government service, for forecasts, observations and alerts. Requests carry a short app identifier and a contact address, as the service requires. Its data practices are described in the NWS privacy policy.
  • NOAA nowCOAST (nowcoast.noaa.gov) for the radar imagery shown in the app.
  • Apple Weather, through Apple's WeatherKit, for worldwide forecasts and the minute-by-minute Next Hour Rain feature in Weather4You Pro. Apple's handling of these requests is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Attribution and legal information is at weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html.

Searching for a city

When you type a place name, the text you type is sent to Apple's Maps services to suggest matching cities and to look up their coordinates and time zones. This works the same way as searching in Apple Maps, and it is covered by Apple's privacy policy. We receive nothing from these searches. Apple Maps is used only to find the place; the weather for it comes from the providers described above.

Saved cities and settings

Your saved cities, temperature units, display style and the most recent forecast are stored on your device so the app opens quickly and the widgets and watch app can show current data. This information is shared only between Weather4You's own app, widgets and watch app on your devices. It is not sent to us.

Weather4You Pro

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds your billing details; we never see them. The app checks with the App Store whether a subscription is active and stores that yes-or-no answer on your device so Pro features work offline for a short time. Apple's terms for subscriptions are at Apple's standard licence agreement.

Notifications

If you turn notifications on, they are scheduled on your device from the forecast the app already has. No notification service of ours is involved and nothing about your notifications leaves the device.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you use a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and usage statistics as described in the TestFlight terms, and you can choose to send us screenshots and comments. We receive only what you send plus Apple's crash reports, and we use them solely to fix the app.

Children

Weather4You is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new effective date. Changes that affect how the app handles data will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
